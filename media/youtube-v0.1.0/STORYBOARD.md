## Video direction

- Palette: use the captured Broadside system exactly—ink black ground, white type, electric blue `#0055FF` as the sole brand accent, and proof green `#20C55A` only for passed gates. Use one flat register per frame; no gradients, shadows, rounded cards, or invented “AI” textures.
- Type and layout: massive lowercase display type carries the argument; IBM Plex Mono is reserved for labels, metrics, commands, and evidence chrome. Every frame has one dominant read, at least three visual depth levels through scale/overlap/opacity, and keeps the bottom 17% clear for captions.
- Motion grammar: smooth long-tail settles, explicit seek-safe `fromTo` entrances, and VO-paced sequential reveals across the whole shot. Never dump the full canvas in the first quarter. No bounce, infinite loops, random motion, lazy breathing, or back-half camera drift. Purposeful station travel in Frame 2 is the only repeated camera move; it locks before the final read.
- Rhythm: Frames 1–3 accumulate momentum; Frame 4 is the dense evidence climax; Frame 5 deliberately slows into the longest readable public-receipt hold. Once a payoff resolves, prefer stillness—at most one finite low-amplitude jitter on a hero.
- Never show: private data, tokens, local filesystem paths, fake upstream UI, browser chrome, nav/footer clutter, slideshow timing, screensaver motion, multi-accent corporate cards, or any claim not supported by the public release evidence.

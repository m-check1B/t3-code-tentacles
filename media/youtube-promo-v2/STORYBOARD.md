## Video direction

- Palette: Broadside exactly — ink black ground, white type, electric blue `#0055FF` as sole accent, proof green `#20C55A` only for passed gates. V2 adds one new register: real white launch-page plates, always presented as physical evidence (hard 1px border, mono provenance label, never full-bleed decoration).
- Type and layout: massive lowercase display type carries the argument; IBM Plex Mono for labels, commands, provenance. One dominant read per frame; bottom 17% stays clear for captions. Site plates are cropped real screenshots — show them big enough to read (hero ≥ 40cqw wide), never as thumbnail confetti.
- Motion grammar: smooth long-tail settles, explicit seek-safe `fromTo` entrances, VO-paced sequential reveals. Plates arrive with a hard-cut or slide-settle and then stay still — evidence does not float. No bounce, no loops, no random drift.
- Rhythm: Frame 1 answers everything in under 6 seconds. Frames 2–3 build mechanism and flows on VO cues. Frame 4 is the dense evidence climax. Frame 5 slows into the longest hold: the URL.
- Never show: private data, tokens, local filesystem paths, fake upstream UI, browser chrome, stale v0.1.0 numbers, or any claim not backed by `assets/PROVENANCE.md`.
